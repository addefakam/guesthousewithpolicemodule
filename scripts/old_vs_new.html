Old System vs. Proposed System
Old Manual System
1
Paper-based guest registration with no centralized database, making data retrieval slow and unreliable
2
No real-time communication between guest houses and police; information shared through phone calls or physical visits
3
No mechanism to cross-reference guest identities against wanted persons or suspect lists
4
Manual room management with hand-written ledgers leading to overbooking and revenue leakage
5
No subscription or compliance tracking; guest houses operate without any regulatory oversight
6
Guests have no way to discover available guest houses or report issues directly
7
No audit trail or accountability for data access by law enforcement or staff
➡
Proposed Digital System
1
Centralized PostgreSQL database with 20+ relational tables capturing every guest, reservation, and transaction
2
Real-time API integration between guest houses and police for instant suspect matching and alerts
3
Automated cross-referencing of every guest check-in against the suspected persons database
4
Digital room and reservation management with availability tracking, payments, and reporting
5
Subscription-based compliance model with admin approval, status tracking, and renewal reminders
6
Mobile PWA for guests to browse houses, view rooms, and report issues directly to police
7
Complete audit logging of every police action with IP tracking and accountability measures
